Ludo

Race all four of your tokens around the board and into your home before anyone else.

Players
⚀
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out. A 6, a capture or getting a token home earns another roll.
  • Land on an opponent to send them back to their base, except on starred and starting squares.
  • You need the exact number to reach home.
